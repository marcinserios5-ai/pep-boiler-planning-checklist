Setting Decision Dates Before a London Consumer Unit Replacement

Build the programme around information

The useful date in a consumer unit replacement in London programme is not only the installation appointment. Assessment, scope agreement and access decisions must happen before it. Put those steps into the schedule so the date is supported by information rather than being a promise made before the work is understood.

For homes in Ealing or Acton, start by listing the records and plans already available. Ask what the electrician needs to review and which questions require a site visit. This establishes a route from first enquiry to an assessed proposal.

Choose dates for owner decisions

In Notting Hill and Chiswick, set a deadline for sharing changes to the refurbishment brief. An agreed kitchen layout or equipment schedule may matter to the electrical discussion. Give the contractor a current version instead of several undated alternatives.

Owners in Kensington or Chelsea should allow time to ask quotation questions before acceptance. Clarify inclusions, exclusions and the treatment of findings in existing circuits. The installation date should follow that understanding.

Confirm people and access

For Hampstead and Maida Vale appointments, schedule a practical check of entry arrangements and areas that need access. Someone must be able to answer questions during the work. Identify that person early rather than relying on whoever happens to be at home.

In St John’s Wood or Fulham, discuss the planned power interruption with the occupants. Ask the electrician to explain the expected programme and what findings could change it. Do not build other commitments around an unconfirmed restoration time.

Leave room for a controlled change

A Hammersmith or Earl’s Court project can include a clear process for additional findings: explain, price where relevant and agree before proceeding. Record who has authority to decide and how they will be contacted.

For Shepherd’s Bush and Queen’s Park homes, put the handover into the programme too. Confirm how the completed scope, equipment information and installation documents will be explained to the owner.
